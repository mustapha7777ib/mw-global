About the company
Who we are

An international trade partner built on reliable sourcing.

Before expanding into international trade, M/W Global Services Limited was established with a simple goal: to build reliable sourcing networks and connect clients with quality products.

We are committed to professionalism, transparency, and delivering products that meet our customers' requirements through strong partnerships and careful supplier selection.

Today M/W Global works across four connected lines — agricultural export, solid minerals, general goods import, and freight and clearing. Clients use one of them or all four. What stays constant is that a single team is accountable from the first enquiry through to delivery.

What we do

Four service lines, one accountable partner.

Most trade problems happen in the handoffs — between the supplier and the inspector, the freight agent and the customs broker. We run all four lines ourselves, so the handoffs are ours to manage.

01
Service 01

Agricultural Commodity Export

Sourcing, consolidation and export of raw and semi-processed agricultural produce.

  • Sourcing through established supplier networks
  • Consolidation to full container volumes
  • Cleaning, sorting and export-standard bagging
  • Pre-shipment inspection arranged where required
  • Export documentation prepared and submitted
02
Service 02

Solid Minerals Trading

Sourcing and export of industrial minerals and ores to buyer specification.

  • Sourcing through vetted mining and processing partners
  • Sorting and grading to the agreed specification
  • Independent assay arranged on request
  • Transport arranged from site to port
  • Export documentation prepared and submitted
03
Service 03

General Goods Import

Procurement and delivery of machinery, industrial equipment, building materials and consumer goods.

  • Supplier identification, vetting and price negotiation
  • Inspection arranged at origin before shipment
  • Import documentation and clearance handled
  • Consolidated shipping for smaller orders
  • Delivery to your warehouse or site
04
Service 04

Freight Forwarding & Clearing

Ocean and air freight, customs clearance, warehousing and inland haulage.

  • Ocean and air freight booking
  • Customs clearance and entry filing
  • Warehousing and stock handling
  • Inland haulage and delivery
  • Cargo insurance arranged on request

Engage one line or the full chain. Either way you deal with one team, one contract and one set of documents. Where a requirement is outside what we can realistically deliver, we say so upfront rather than agreeing to it and arguing later.

How we work
How we work

From first enquiry to documents in hand

01

Enquiry

You tell us the commodity, grade, volume and destination. We come back with an indicative offer.

02

Contract & Terms

We agree the specification, Incoterms and payment arrangement, then issue a sales contract and proforma invoice.

03

Sourcing & Checks

We source and consolidate the order, and check it against the agreed specification before loading.

04

Shipment & Documents

We book space, load and ship, and hand over the document set the buyer needs to take delivery.

Documentation we prepare

Most trade disputes are paperwork disputes. The document set is assembled to match the contract and the destination's import requirements, and issued complete.

  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Bill of lading / air waybill
  • Certificate of origin
  • Phytosanitary certificate (agricultural lots)
  • Quality / weight certificate
  • Independent inspection or assay report
  • Fumigation certificate where required
  • Insurance certificate where arranged

Document requirements vary by commodity and destination. We confirm the exact set at contract stage so nothing is discovered late.
